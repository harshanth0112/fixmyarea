Our Mission

Built for better, safer, and cleaner neighborhoods.

FixMyArea is a community-first civic technology concept designed to make neighborhood problems easier to discover, document, and follow through to resolution.

1. Visibility

Make everyday hazards like unlit street corners, broken pavements, and blocked drains visible so they are never forgotten.

2. Community

Encourage residents to actively participate in neighborhood stewardship through simple, constructive reporting.

3. Transparency

Provide an unambiguous status lifecycle so community members know exactly where every issue stands.

Why this demonstration exists

FixMyArea was conceived as a clean, frontend-focused demonstration of modern editorial web application design inspired by platforms like the Ellipsus Help Center.

It features zero backend dependencies, running entirely within modern browsers using vanilla JavaScript modules and Tailwind CSS. All reports, timelines, and statistics presented across this platform are purely simulated.

Important Disclaimer: FixMyArea is not an official government portal and does not submit data to municipal authorities.
